## What you'll find here

- **DSA** — LeetCode & Striver (TakeUForward) problems grouped by pattern, each with clean JavaScript/Java solutions and an **interactive step-by-step dry run** you can click through.

Every problem follows the same layout — statement, solution, visual dry run, and test cases — so it's easy to revise quickly. Happy studying!
